Diego's Scholarship Dashboard
=============================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Double-click "index.html". It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, etc.).
No installs, no terminal, nothing to set up.

  TIP: The very first time you open it, be connected to the internet
  (it loads its styling from the web once, then works fine). After that
  it works offline too.


WHAT IT DOES
------------
- Scholarships tab: every scholarship matched to Diego (Howard sophomore,
  Computer Engineering, Black + Hispanic/Latino, NSBE VP, financial need).
  Each card shows the award amount, deadline, WHY it fits him, things to
  watch out for (like citizenship rules), and an "Apply" button that opens
  the official site. Filter by category, "eligible now," or hide the ones
  that require U.S. citizenship. Sort by deadline or amount.

- For each scholarship you can set a status (Interested / In progress /
  Submitted / Awarded / Not selected) and jot private notes.

- Deadlines tab: everything laid out by date so nothing slips.

- Essay Locker tab: Diego's own essays are pre-loaded so he can copy-paste
  and tailor them fast. He can add, edit, or delete essays.


WHERE YOUR PROGRESS IS SAVED
----------------------------
Statuses, notes, and essays are saved automatically inside the browser on
THIS computer. They do NOT sync to other devices. If you want Diego to have
his own copy, send him this whole "diegos-dashboard" folder and he opens it
on his machine.


IMPORTANT
---------
Scholarship amounts and deadlines change every year. The dates shown are the
next realistic deadline or a typical window. ALWAYS confirm the real details
on each scholarship's official page (the "Apply" button) before applying.
